# Staff AI desktop downloads

Staff for Mac is in beta. Get it from the [latest release](https://github.com/trystaff/staff-downloads/releases/latest)
or from [trystaff.ai/download](https://trystaff.ai/download). Windows is coming later.

Staff AI brings your AI team to your Mac: hire people, give them work, watch the office. Sign in with your Staff
account and everyone runs on your team's credit; connect a Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan of your own in Settings if you
have one, and it is used until it runs out.

## Install

1. Download the disk image for your Mac: **Apple Silicon** (M1 and later) or **Intel**. Apple menu → About This Mac
   says which chip you have.
2. Open the disk image and drag **Staff** into Applications.
3. This beta is not yet notarized with Apple, so the first launch needs one extra step: open Staff once (macOS says it
   cannot verify the developer), then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and press **Open Anyway**
   next to Staff. After that it opens like any app.
4. Staff opens your office in a window. Press **Settings** and sign in with your Staff account.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Internet access and a Staff account are required.

## Checksums

Every release carries `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Verify a download with `shasum -a 256 <file>` and compare.

Download only from this official repository or trystaff.ai.

This repository distributes installer binaries. It does not contain application source.
